# Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The hospitality industry faces significant challenges due to hotel booking cancellations, which directly impact revenue generation, room occupancy, and operational efficiency. This project analyzes hotel booking data from both City Hotel and Resort Hotel properties to identify the key factors influencing booking cancellations and provide actionable business recommendations.

The analysis is based on a dataset containing **119,390 hotel booking records** from **July 2015 to August 2017**, including both completed and canceled reservations.

## Business Problem

Both City Hotels and Resort Hotels have experienced high cancellation rates, leading to:

* Revenue loss due to unoccupied rooms.
* Inefficient room allocation and planning.
* Reduced operational efficiency.
* Challenges in pricing and promotional decision-making.

The primary objective of this project is to understand the drivers behind booking cancellations and suggest data-driven strategies to reduce cancellation rates and improve hotel profitability.

## Objectives

* Analyze hotel booking cancellation patterns.
* Identify factors that contribute to reservation cancellations.
* Evaluate the impact of pricing on cancellation behavior.
* Understand customer booking channels and market segments.
* Provide recommendations to optimize revenue and occupancy rates.
* Create an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.

## Assumptions

* No major external events significantly affected hotel operations during the analysis period.
* The historical data accurately reflects customer booking behavior.
* Hotels are not currently implementing the recommended strategies.
* Booking cancellations are one of the major factors affecting revenue generation.
* Canceled reservations result in vacant rooms and lost revenue opportunities.

## Research Questions

1. What factors influence hotel booking cancellations?
2. How can cancellation rates be reduced?
3. How can hotels optimize pricing and promotional strategies?
4. Which customer segments contribute most to cancellations?
5. What booking channels generate the highest cancellation rates?

## Hypotheses

* Higher room prices lead to increased booking cancellations.
* Longer lead times are associated with higher cancellation rates.
* Customers booking through travel agents contribute significantly to cancellations.
* Certain market segments are more likely to cancel reservations.
* Resort and City Hotels exhibit different cancellation behaviors.

## Key Insights

* Cancellation rates increase as room prices rise.
* Online and offline travel agencies contribute a significant portion of bookings.
* Longer lead times generally result in higher cancellation probability.
* City Hotels experience higher cancellation rates compared to Resort Hotels.
* Pricing strategy has a direct impact on booking retention.

* Implement dynamic pricing strategies.
* Offer non-refundable or partially refundable booking options.
* Provide loyalty discounts to repeat customers.
* Monitor high-risk cancellation segments.
* Optimize marketing campaigns based on customer behavior patterns.
* Reduce dependency on channels with consistently high cancellation rates.
